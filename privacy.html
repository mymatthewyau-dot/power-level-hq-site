Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Power Level HQ is a personal app used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Calendar, with his permission, to show events and to create or edit events he asks for.

  • Calendar data is processed only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on that computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
